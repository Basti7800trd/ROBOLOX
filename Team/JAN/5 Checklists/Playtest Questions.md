# Playtest Questions

Part of [[Roblox Home]]. Claude prewrites these with every change and asks them in popups after a test ("Did X work? yes / no / other"), up to 4 per popup. Answered questions go into the changelog entry; open ones stay here.

## Open: the big update (Roblox Changelog (11)-(12), 2026-10-25)
Popup A (first minutes):
- Did the tutorial (quest pill at the top) always make clear what to do next, up to the first drone?
- Did the guide beam / arrow point to the right station?
- Do the signs over the oven, fridge and prep table show ABOVE them (not inside or below)? If not: Claude flips `SIGN_ANCHOR` / `STATUS_ANCHOR` in Stations.
- Do the customer bubbles (order + patience ring) show above their heads?

Popup B (money + goals):
- Did the stars go up when you served fast, and did customers come faster with more stars?
- Was unlocking a recipe in the Recipe Book clear (price + stars needed)?
- Did buying + placing furniture in BUILD feel easy (shop list, ghost, turn, store)?
- Did the drones (from the dock) do their jobs without getting stuck?

Popup C (look + feel):
- Does the new world look good (hills, lake, waterfall, trees, title sign, day/night)?
- Is the new HUD (6 big buttons, money, stars, next goal) clear and not too full?
- Are the sounds and the day music nice, or too loud / annoying?
- On a phone: does everything fit, and can you tap every button?

Popup D (saving + boards, after "Enable Studio Access to API Services" or in the published game):
- Leave + join again: is everything still there (money, restaurant, fridge, drones, quests)?
- After a while away: did "Welcome back!" pay offline drone money?
- Do the 3 lobby boards show your name (top earners, pizzas per minute, fastest pizza)?
- With a friend in the same server: do you each get your own restaurant, and do customers go to the right one?

## Open: beta start (Roblox Changelog (6), 2026-10-25)
Popup 1 (the loop):
- Did you get your own restaurant with your name on the sign?
- Could you order on the PC in the office and pick up the boxes in the garage?
- Did storing boxes in the fridge, cutting and the prep table feel clear?
- Did the oven bake the pizza (and burn it when you waited too long)?

Popup 2 (customers + money):
- Did customers sit only on chairs at tables, order, and pay you when served?
- Did a customer leave angry when you were too slow?
- Is the money (bottom left) and the leaderboard in the lobby right?
- Did anything feel too slow or too fast (delivery 15 s, bake 12 s, patience 90 s)?

Popup 3 (look + feel):
- Does the style feel close enough to "Automate a Restaurant"?
- Are the buttons on the left (OPEN / HOME / MENU) and the hint line clear?
- On a phone: is everything big enough to tap?
- What should come next: build mode, saving, or the first drone?
